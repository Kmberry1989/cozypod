# Hats and front accessories

- **Canvas:** exactly 256×256 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
- **Registration:** keep the original 256×256 canvas; do not trim transparent
  edges. Align to a body centered on x=128, with the top of the body near y=48.
- **Safe bounds:** x=12–244 and y=8–220. Leave the lower 36 px transparent.
- **Layering:** this folder renders in front of the face and body. Use it for
  hats, glasses, masks, bangs, headphones, or foreground costume details.
- **Art direction:** handmade stop-motion clay, softly rounded forms, visible but
  restrained fingerprints, warm upper-left key light, subtle lower-right shadow.
- **Do not include:** a Podling body, face, room background, text, UI, baked drop
  shadow outside the accessory, or a colored canvas.
