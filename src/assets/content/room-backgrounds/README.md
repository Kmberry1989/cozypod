# Room backgrounds

- **Canvas:** 1120×1400 px portrait (4:5) preferred; minimum 840×1050.
- **Safe areas:** keep the top 130 px calm for room controls, the center x=110–1010
  open for four Podlings, and the lower 270 px low-contrast for the action rail.
- **Composition:** eye-level clay diorama room with a clear floor plane and four
  distinct standing zones. Important landmarks must survive a center crop.
- **Art direction:** warm tactile stop-motion clay, walnut/cocoa/parchment with
  tomato, butter, mint, lilac, and sky accents; rainy-night warmth by default.
- **Do not include:** characters, text, logos, UI, phone frames, color overlays,
  or baked controls.
- **Unlocks:** new project backgrounds automatically unlock at levels 4, 8,
  12, and so on, in alphabetical filename order. The three included reward
  environments keep their named level or trophy requirements.
