# Trophies

- **Canvas:** exactly 512×512 px, transparent PNG or WebP; do not crop the base.
- **Registration:** center the trophy on x=256 and keep the full silhouette
  within x=36–476 and y=24–492.
- **Safe bounds:** leave at least 24 px of transparent padding on every edge.
- **Art direction:** warm stop-motion polymer clay, collectible toy proportions,
  rounded edges, subtle fingerprints, warm upper-left key light, and a soft
  lower-right shadow. Use the CozyPod coral, mint, moss, lilac, cocoa, and gold
  palette.
- **Do not include:** scenery, text, UI, a colored canvas, or a Podling body.
- **Unlocks:** trophies appear on the Wardrobe shelf automatically. One trophy
  unlocks for each trophy the player earns, in alphabetical filename order.
